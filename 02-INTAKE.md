# Intake

**This is the most important file in the pack.** Everything the AI writes is built
on it. An empty intake produces generic content with invented facts. A filled one
produces content only you could have written.

Takes about 30 minutes. Do it once, then update when things change.

> **Rule: if you cannot prove it, do not write it here.** This file is the list of
> things that are allowed to appear in public. If a number is a guess, mark it a
> guess or leave it out.

## Worked example (delete this section)

To show the level of detail that makes a difference:

> **What they believe that is wrong:** Founders think a redesign is a visual
> project. It is a sales project. They come to us for a prettier site and leave
> with a shorter sales cycle.
>
> **What they are quietly afraid of:** That an enterprise buyer will open their
> site, decide they look like a two-person startup, and never reply — and that
> they will never find out this is why.
>
> **Language we own:** "design entropy" — the way a design system quietly falls
> apart over 12 months unless the team is trained to maintain it.

Three sentences like that produce better content than fifty pages of brand guidelines.
