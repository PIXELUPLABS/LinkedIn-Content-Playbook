## 1. The basics

- **Who ships the posts:** Daksh Aswal (founder) at 4x/week, plus team members in
  their own lanes per `12-TEAM-CONTENT.md`. Each person writes through their own
  voice bible; this file is the shared fact base underneath all of them.
- **Company:** Pixelup Labs
- **What we do, in one sentence a customer would use:** We make B2B startups look
  enterprise-ready — so buyers stop hesitating and deals stop stalling.
- **What we do, the longer version:** Positioning-led brand identity, website,
  product design, and design systems for funded B2B startups (most of them
  AI-native) going upmarket — built around the opinion the founder actually holds
  about their market, shipped in weeks not quarters, with the system and training
  left behind so it doesn't decay when we're gone.
- **What we do NOT do:** Growth/demand-gen (no leads, SEO, or ads — "we fix how
  you're perceived; your growth team fights the volume war"). No pixel-pushing
  execution of someone else's locked Figma ("we're not a pair of hands"). No
  committee-driven enterprise rebrand programs — we work founder-to-founder.

## 2. Who we are talking to

- **Primary buyer ("Arjun", the Carol):** Founder/CEO or co-founder of an
  AI-native B2B software company (infra, devtools, vertical AI). Series A–B,
  $5M–60M raised, 10–80 employees, SF/US/UK/EU. Just hired their first enterprise
  AE; a "Contact sales" tier quietly appeared on the pricing page. Technical,
  design-aware but not design-led, opinionated about their market, decides fast
  with founder gut — no procurement. Secondary: Head of Growth/COO at Series A–C
  B2B startups hitting the same wall.
- **The moment they need us (inciting events — sell to the date):**
  8 of 10 discovery calls arrived with a dated event. The triggers, in order:
  a lost or stalled enterprise deal ("buyer went quiet after security review");
  a fresh Series A/B raise with upmarket expectations attached; a competitor
  shipping a beautiful rebrand; an investor or prospect saying "your site doesn't
  do you justice"; the first enterprise AE hired and embarrassed by the deck;
  a launch/fundraise/demo-day deadline ~8 weeks out; a 12-month-old rebrand
  visibly decaying.
- **Who we are NOT for (say this out loud — it's the filter):** Pre-PMF and
  pre-seed founders ("use a template and Claude Code, honestly — come back after
  the pivot settles"). Rebrand-by-committee buyers (a Character- or Landor-tier
  firm will serve them better). Cost-shoppers. Buyers expecting design to drive
  MQLs. Founders with a locked Figma who want executors.
- **What they believe that is wrong:** That a redesign is a visual project. It is
  perception engineering: enterprise buyers aren't buying beauty, they're buying
  the absence of risk, and design is how risk-absence is communicated before
  anyone reads a word. Also wrong: that design is decoration rather than the
  limiting reagent — never the whole reaction, but the thing that caps how far
  every other GTM investment can go.
- **What they are quietly afraid of:** That an enterprise buyer will open their
  site, conclude "these guys might disappear in six months," and never reply —
  and they will never find out that this was why. Their real state is
  *inarticulate certainty*: they know good design when they feel it, they know
  their own site isn't it, and they cannot write the brief. In their words:
  "you go to these premium sites and you feel it" · "make us look more legit and
  developed than we are" · somewhere along the way, the face stopped matching
  the back end.

## 4. What we must never claim

- **Prices or packages:** nothing is publicly launched and the surfaces currently
  contradict each other (Contra vs. mainline). No numbers in posts until pricing
  is unified and published.
- **Turnaround guarantees:** "we work to your date" is a stance; "done in X weeks
  guaranteed" is a landmine. Past timelines are receipts, never promises.
- **Metric guarantees:** never promise business outcomes (deals closed, demo
  lift, raise success). Show them as case-study evidence, don't promise them.
  Daksh's own line: "if an agency promises to transform your product metrics in
  a 2-week sprint, RUN."
- **"More legit than you are":** buyer language, but the wrong side of the trust
  story. Our stance is always "as legit as you actually are, finally visible."
  Never promise to make anyone look better than they are.
- **Unscoped Greptile credit:** claim "2025 rebrand + design system," dated.
  Greptile's April 2026 refresh was in-house. Do not lead outbound or posts with
  Greptile until the attribution is re-confirmed by the client.
- **The category line:** "the enterprise-readiness design partner for B2B
  startups" is drafted but **not yet signed off by Daksh**. Do not ship it as a
  tagline until it is.
- **"AI companies" as the audience label:** say "B2B startups" (with AI-heavy
  proof), per the positioning doc's wording resolution.
