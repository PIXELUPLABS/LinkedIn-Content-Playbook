# The Hook Library

Roughly 260 hooks, sorted by type. Every one is under the 78-character sweet spot
or close to it.

**How to use this.** Do not copy a line. Find the *shape* that fits what you have
to say, then rebuild it with your own number, your own client, your own week.
A borrowed hook with a borrowed number is the fastest way to sound like everyone else.

Examples are written for a design and brand agency, because a filled example teaches
more than a blank template. The template above each block is the portable part.

> **Every `[bracket]` needs a real number or a real name.** If you cannot fill it
> from `02-INTAKE.md`, pick a different hook.

> ⚠️ **A warning about milestone and money posts.** In one design agency's real
> data, revenue-milestone posts scored highest on engagement — and that engagement
> came mostly from peers, students and people in the founder's home market, not from
> buyers. Money posts reliably win the wrong audience. Use them once or twice a
> month at most, and never as the main engine. See `11-MEASUREMENT.md`.

## The ten hook openings to retire immediately

These are not weak. They actively cost you reach.

251. ❌ "Excited to announce…"
252. ❌ "Thrilled to share…"
253. ❌ "Humbled and honoured…"
254. ❌ "Ever wondered why…?" *(open-ended question with no answer coming)*
255. ❌ "In today's fast-paced world…"
256. ❌ "Let that sink in."
257. ❌ "Here's a thread 🧵" *(wrong platform)*
258. ❌ "Agree?" / "Thoughts?" as an opener
259. ❌ "Unpopular opinion:" *(now so common it signals a popular opinion)*
260. ❌ "You won't believe what happened next." *(the withheld payoff — see `04-HOOK-GUIDE.md` Part 4)*
