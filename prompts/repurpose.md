# Repurpose a winner

This post worked. Get four more things out of it.

**The post:**
```
[PASTE]
```
**What it did:** [impressions / saves / shares / comments / what happened]

**Give me:**

**1. The longer version.** What did the post skip? Expand into a playbook (F1) with
the detail the original could not fit.

**2. The shorter version.** The single sharpest idea, as a one-liner plus an asset
(F13). Under 100 characters of text.

**3. The opposite.** Every strong opinion has a defensible antithesis. Argue against
this post honestly — not a strawman. What is the strongest case for the other side,
and when is it right?

**4. A different vantage point.** Rewrite it from the perspective of a different role
on the team — a designer, a project lead, an engineer. Same idea, their angle, their
evidence. (See `12-TEAM-CONTENT.md`, Model B.)

**5. Other channels.** How this becomes: a newsletter section, a 45-second video
script, and a carousel.

**Rules:**
- **Do not re-post the same post.** It worked because it was new, and that is spent.
- Each version must stand alone. Someone who saw the original should still get
  something from the new one.
- Space these out. Not the same week, and check the 90-day rule in
  `03-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`.
