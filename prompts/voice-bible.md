# Build a voice bible

Run this **once per person** you write for. It is the highest-value prompt in the pack.

**Input:** paste 30–60 of their real posts. More is better. If you have engagement
numbers, include them.

```
[PASTE POSTS]
```

**Important:** if you have engagement data, **analyse their top 30% separately** and
tell me where the winners differ from the average. Never build a voice guide from
someone's average output.

---

**Produce a document with these sections. Be forensic, not flattering. Quote
constantly — a voice guide with no verbatim evidence is a description, not a guide.**

**§1. The voice in one paragraph.** If a stranger read only this, could they fake it?

**§2. The hard rules.** Things true in almost every post. Test each against a holdout
sample and say how many posts it held for. Only list rules that survive.

**§3. The registers.** Do they write in more than one mode? Name each, say what
triggers it, and give an example.

**§4. The format library.** Every recurring post shape, with:
- a skeleton, line by line
- roughly what share of their output it is
- a real example

**§5. Hooks.** Their opening moves, ranked by how often they use them. Quote at least
three real examples of each. Note anything they *never* do.

**§6. Endings.** How posts land, ranked by frequency, with real examples.

**§7. Micro-style — the fingerprint.** This is the section that matters most:
- typical sentence length
- "I" or "we", and when it switches
- punctuation habits — dashes, ellipses, parentheses, line breaks
- capitalisation habits, including inconsistencies (do not tidy these — they are the fingerprint)
- emoji use, exactly
- do they swear? where?
- their tics — the words and constructions they reach for repeatedly

**§8. Lexicon.** Words they use constantly. Words they never use. Their coined terms.

**§9. Numbers.** How they use figures. Do they round? Do they lead with them? What
share of posts carry one?

**§10. Story moves.** How they open a story, how they build tension, how they land it.

**§11. Evolution.** How the voice changed over time. Write in the *current* voice, not
the average of all eras. Say what changed and roughly when.

**§12. What the low performers teach.** What is present in their worst posts and
absent from their best?

**§13. The impostor test.** A checklist someone can run on a draft to answer: would a
regular reader notice this was not them?

**§14. Generation checklist.** A mechanical, in-order list for writing a new post in
this voice.

---

**Then validate it.** Take 5 posts you did NOT analyse. Predict what the guide says
they should look like, then compare. Tell me honestly where the guide failed. A voice
guide that has not been tested against a holdout is a guess.
