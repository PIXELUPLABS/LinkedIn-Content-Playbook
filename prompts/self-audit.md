# Audit my account

Here is my LinkedIn analytics export.

```
[PASTE THE EXPORT, OR THE TOP ~50 POSTS WITH THEIR NUMBERS]
```

**Important before you start:**
- If any of these posts were boosted or promoted, tell me — those impressions are
  paid and they will corrupt the whole analysis. Exclude them or flag them.
- Ignore anything below 30 posts of data. Say so rather than guessing.

**Do this:**

**1. Classify every post.** Add these columns:
- Hook type (from `04-HOOK-GUIDE.md` Part 5)
- Format (from `06-POST-STRUCTURE.md`)
- Media type (text / image / carousel / video)
- Character count
- Hook character count
- Topic

**2. Split the top 30% from the bottom 30%.** Discard the middle — it is noise.

**3. Answer these five questions with evidence:**
- What do the top 30% have in common that the bottom 30% do not?
- Which hook type appears most in the top? Which appears most in the bottom?
- Is there a length pattern? Give me the quartiles.
- Which media type performs best? By how much?
- Which topic reliably underperforms?

**4. Check the audience question.** Based on who engaged, does my engagement look
like it is coming from buyers or from peers? If you cannot tell from the data, say
so and tell me how to check. (See `11-MEASUREMENT.md` §8 — this is where most
accounts are quietly optimising for the wrong crowd.)

**5. Separate the scoreboards.** Which posts won reach, which won engagement rate,
and which produced saves, shares or tag-a-colleague comments? Do not merge them —
they are three different results.

**Then give me:**

```
THE ONE CHANGE: the single highest-leverage thing to change next month
WHY: the evidence
WHAT TO STOP: the format or topic to drop entirely
WHAT TO DOUBLE: the format or topic to do twice as much of
CONFIDENCE: honest — how much of this is signal and how much is a small sample?
```

One change. Not five. Then I re-run this next month and see if it moved.
