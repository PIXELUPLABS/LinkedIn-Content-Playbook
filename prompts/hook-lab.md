# Hook lab

The body of this post is fine. The opening is not.

**The post:**
```
[PASTE THE FULL POST]
```

**Do this:**

1. **Find the buried hook.** Read the whole post and tell me the most surprising
   sentence anywhere in it — usually it is in the middle, not the top. Quote it.

2. **Write 10 new hooks.** Use ten *different* types from Part 5 of
   `04-HOOK-GUIDE.md`. Label each one with its type. Every one under 78 characters.

3. **Score each** on:
   - Would it stop a scroll? (1–5)
   - Does the post actually deliver what the hook promises? (yes/no)
   - Which scoreboard does it optimise for — reach, resonance, or revenue?

4. **Recommend one**, and say what it costs. Every hook trades something away —
   name the trade.

5. **Show me the rewritten line 2** for your recommendation. The payoff has to land
   immediately, and changing the hook usually breaks the second line.

**Rules:** no round numbers. No "excited to announce." No withheld payoff. No
open-ended question unless you answer it in line 2.
