# Turn this into a carousel

**The source:**
```
[PASTE A POST, A PLAYBOOK, OR A LIST OF POINTS]
```

**Build it to the anatomy in `08-MEDIA-AND-IMAGES.md` §4:**

```
Slide 1     THE HOOK — big type, one idea, nothing else
Slide 2     THE STAKES — why this matters, one sentence
Slides 3-8  ONE IDEA EACH — never two
Slide 9     THE SUMMARY — every point on one screen (this is the slide people screenshot)
Slide 10    THE ASK — who I am, what I do, one soft next step
```

**For each slide give me:**
- **Headline** — under 8 words
- **Body** — under 25 words, or none at all
- **Visual note** — what should be on the slide besides text

**Rules:**
- 8–12 slides total
- Same type size on every slide
- Number them (3/9)
- Slide 1 is a hook with a picture — hold it to the same standard as a post hook
- Slide 9 is the one that gets saved. Make it the densest and clearest.

**Then write the accompanying post text**, 300–700 characters, that sits above the
carousel. It should make someone swipe, not repeat what the carousel says.
