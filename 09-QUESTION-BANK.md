# The Question Bank

**The most valuable file in this pack.**

Nobody can "come up with a post." Everybody can answer a question. This bank turns
the hardest part of content — having something to say — into something you can do
out loud, in two minutes, on a walk.

---

## How to use it

**The wrong way:** read a question, open a doc, try to write a post.

**The right way:**
1. Pick a question. Any question. Do not optimise the choice.
2. **Answer it out loud into a voice memo.** Two to four minutes. Do not prepare.
3. Get it transcribed.
4. Hand the transcript to your AI with `prompts/draft-post.md`.

**Speaking beats writing, every time.** You are more specific, more opinionated and
more human out loud than you will ever be typing into an empty document. The messy
transcript is the raw material; the AI cleans it up.

**One good answer is 3–5 posts**, not one. A single question usually produces a
playbook, a story, a contrarian take and a carousel.

### The rules

- **Answer with a specific story, not a general view.** If your answer has no date,
  no name and no number in it, answer again.
- **Extract the rule, then attach the story.** A story is not an insight — it is the
  *proof* of one. The post is the rule; the story is why anyone should believe it.
- **Mark questions you cannot answer.** That is not a failure. It tells you where you
  have no position yet, which is useful information about the business.
- **Never answer a question twice in 90 days.**

### Tags

- 🔑 **Founder-only** — nobody else in the building can answer this
- 👥 **Anyone** — any team member can answer it well
- 💰 **High intent** — attracts buyers, not peers. Prioritise these.
- 📣 **High reach** — attracts a crowd. Good for the top of the funnel.

# Section A — The Founder (1–90)

## Origin and the arc

1. 🔑 What were you doing right before you started this, and what made you stop? 📣
2. 🔑 What did you believe about this business on day one that turned out to be wrong?
3. 🔑 What was the first project you were genuinely proud of, and what did it pay?

## When the bank runs dry

It will not, but if it feels like it does:

- **Take any question and ask it about someone else.** "What do great clients do
  differently?" becomes "What do great agencies do differently?"
- **Take any answer and argue the opposite.** Every strong opinion has a defensible
  antithesis, and it is a post.
- **Take any question and add a constraint.** "How do you price?" becomes "How would
  you price if you had to close in one call?"
- **Read your own comments.** The question someone asked under last month's post is
  the best question in this bank, because a real person asked it.
- **Read your sales call transcripts.** Every objection is a question. Every question
  asked three times is a post.
