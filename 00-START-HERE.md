# The LinkedIn Content Engine

**A complete system for a founder and their team to write LinkedIn content that
brings in customers — not just likes.**

This is not a list of tips. It is a working system. It has the rules, the hooks,
the structures, the questions to ask yourself, and the prompts to run it with an
AI assistant.

---

## What makes this different

Most LinkedIn advice is one person's opinion about what worked for them once.

Everything in this pack comes from one of four places, and each rule says which:

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| **MEASURED** | A year of one B2B founder's LinkedIn account (~230 posts), where every post was scored against real platform analytics. Paid promotion was stripped out before measuring, so the numbers show what people actually chose to read. |
| **CROSS-CHECK** | An independent study of 559 posts from three well-known B2B creators. Where this agrees with the measured set, trust it hard. Where it disagrees, the pack says so. |
| **YOUR OWN DATA** | The method for measuring your own account, which beats any outside benchmark. See `11-MEASUREMENT.md`. |
| **PRACTITIONER** | Rules learned by running a founder's account to real revenue over two years. Marked as judgement, not proof. |

**Ratios are honest. Absolute numbers are not portable.** You will see "1.6x
better than average" and not "412,000 impressions." A ratio transfers to your
account. Someone else's follower count does not.

## The one-paragraph version

Your hook decides whether anything else gets read. Short posts beat long ones.
Text-only posts lose badly — attach something to look at. Reach, engagement and
leads are three different scoreboards that pull in different directions, so pick
which one you are playing before you write. Never start from a blank page: keep a
bank of ideas and a bank of questions, and answer a question out loud instead of
trying to "come up with a post." Post four times a week, not seven. Judge the
account after three months, not three weeks.

## How to use this pack

### If you have 20 minutes
Read `04-HOOK-GUIDE.md` and `07-WRITING-STYLE.md`. Those two change your next post.

### If you are setting the system up (do this once, ~90 minutes)
1. Fill in `02-INTAKE.md`. Nothing else works properly until this is done. It is
   the file that makes every AI output sound like *you* instead of like everyone.
2. Fix your profile using `13-PROFILE-FOUNDATION.md`. It is a one-time job and it
   is the cheapest win in the pack.
3. Read `03-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md` and pick your weekly rhythm.
4. Book the first capture session using `10-INTERVIEW-PROTOCOL.md`.

### If you are writing today
- Stuck on the opening → `04-HOOK-GUIDE.md`, then `05-HOOK-LIBRARY.md`
- Stuck on what to say → `09-QUESTION-BANK.md`, pick any question, answer it out loud
- Have the words, need the shape → `06-POST-STRUCTURE.md`
- Have the post, need the picture → `08-MEDIA-AND-IMAGES.md`
- Want a blunt second opinion → run `prompts/critique.md`

### If you are the team lead
Read `12-TEAM-CONTENT.md`. Getting five people posting is a completely different
problem from getting one founder posting, and almost nobody has a real process
for it.

## Setting this up inside Claude (or any AI assistant)

1. Create a new Project.
2. Upload every file in this folder to the Project's knowledge.
3. Copy the contents of `01-PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the Project's custom
   instructions box.
4. Fill in `02-INTAKE.md` **before** you upload it. An empty intake makes the AI
   invent facts about you, and invented facts are the fastest way to lose trust.
5. Start a chat with any prompt from `prompts/`.

Re-upload `02-INTAKE.md` whenever your positioning, pricing or proof changes.

## Three warnings before you start

**1. Content compounds. It does not convert on demand.**
The account that brings in work in month nine looks broken in month two. If you
judge it weekly you will kill it before it works. Set the review at 90 days.

**2. Marketing manufactures luck. It is not a vending machine.**
A prospect sees your work, sends it to a friend, and that friend calls you three
weeks later. No dashboard will ever show you that path. Do not judge content the
way you judge an ad.

**3. Impressions are the wrong goal for most businesses.**
If you sell to businesses, likes are a vanity number. `11-MEASUREMENT.md` shows
you the three signals that actually predict a lead, and none of them is likes.
